# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## UX/Design Lead

### Role Summary
UX/Design Leads ensure products are usable, accessible, and aligned to customer needs. They translate user insights into clear design direction and work closely with product and engineering teams.

### Responsibilities
- Lead user research, design reviews, and interaction decisions
- Define information architecture, flows, and UX requirements
- Partner with Product Managers on problem framing and success criteria
- Collaborate with Developers and QA to validate usability, accessibility, and edge cases
- Help prioritize design trade-offs and customer experience risks

### Goals
- Improve usability and customer satisfaction
- Reduce misalignment between product intent and implementation
- Create designs that are accessible, testable, and easy to support

### Typical Communication
- Product discovery and design critiques
- UX review sessions and acceptance criteria discussions
- Handoffs to Developers and QA for implementation validation

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Product Managers to turn customer needs into experience goals and requirements.
- Provides design direction to Developers and QA to ensure implementation matches the intended experience.
- Keeps Project Managers informed of design risks, dependencies, and milestones that could affect delivery.

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads or Architects guide solution design, platform choices, and technical trade-offs. They help teams balance delivery speed with maintainability, reliability, and long-term scalability.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical direction, architecture patterns, and integration approaches
- Review design decisions, technical risks, and dependencies
- Support estimation and prioritization with Project Managers and Developers
- Guide implementation quality, code standards, and maintainability
- Partner with Security and Operations stakeholders on risk, reliability, and readiness

### Goals
- Deliver robust, maintainable solutions
- Reduce technical debt and avoid avoidable rework
- Enable delivery teams to make informed architectural decisions

### Typical Communication
- Architecture reviews and technical design discussions
- Dependency planning and milestone risk reviews
- Handoffs with Security, QA, and Operations for readiness checks

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers to shape implementation approach and review technical quality.
- Advises Product Managers and Project Managers on feasibility, sequencing, and dependencies.
- Coordinates with Security/Privacy and Operations leads on production readiness and operational concerns.

---

## Data / Analytics Lead

### Role Summary
Data and Analytics Leads define measurement plans, instrumentation needs, and evidence-gathering approaches. They ensure teams can evaluate whether the project is delivering the intended outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define success metrics, dashboards, and instrumentation requirements
- Partner with Product Managers on outcome validation and reporting
- Work with Developers and QA to confirm event tracking and data quality
- Identify gaps in measurement or monitoring that affect decision-making
- Support post-launch analysis and improvement opportunities

### Goals
- Make product decisions evidence-based
- Improve visibility into customer and business impact
- Ensure metrics are trustworthy, actionable, and aligned to goals

### Typical Communication
- KPI definition and dashboard reviews
- Data quality checks and reporting discussions
- Post-release measurement reviews with stakeholders

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Collaborates with Product Managers to define success metrics and outcome thresholds.
- Partners with Developers and QA to confirm analytics implementation and validation coverage.
- Shares findings with Project Managers and stakeholders to support status updates and decisions.

---

## Security / Privacy Lead

### Role Summary
Security and Privacy Leads identify risks, controls, and compliance requirements needed to protect customer and business data. They ensure security and privacy are considered throughout the project lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Review security and privacy requirements and threat considerations
- Evaluate dependencies, controls, and risk mitigation actions
- Partner with engineering and delivery teams on remediation and verification
- Escalate material risks and coordinate response planning
- Contribute to release readiness and post-incident learning

### Goals
- Reduce avoidable risk to customers and the business
- Embed secure-by-default practices in delivery work
- Maintain trust in how data is handled and protected

### Typical Communication
- Security reviews, risk assessments, and remediation planning
- Release readiness check-ins and incident escalation paths
- Cross-functional decision-making with Product, Engineering, and Operations leads

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers and QA to review vulnerabilities, controls, and validation needs.
- Advises Project Managers and Product Managers on risk implications and mitigation priorities.
- Coordinates with Technical Leads and Operations for production readiness and incident response support.

---

## Operations / SRE / Release Manager

### Role Summary
Operations, SRE, and Release Managers help ensure systems are reliable, observable, and ready for safe deployment. They focus on service health, release sequencing, and recovery readiness.

### Responsibilities
- Support deployment planning, monitoring, and rollback readiness
- Review system reliability, alerting, and operational dependencies
- Coordinate smoke tests, release windows, and post-deploy verification
- Help maintain runbooks, service health checks, and incident response readiness
- Partner with Product and Project Managers on communication and change impact

### Goals
- Minimize deployment risk and service disruption
- Improve reliability and operational clarity
- Ensure teams can recover quickly when issues arise

### Typical Communication
- Deployment planning and release readiness reviews
- Post-release verification and incident coordination
- Operational updates with engineering and stakeholder teams

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Aligns with Developers and Technical Leads on release sequencing, monitoring needs, and rollback decisions.
- Works with QA to confirm smoke tests and production verification criteria.
- Supports Project Managers by keeping release timelines, dependencies, and operational risks visible.

---

## Customer Support / Success Representative

### Role Summary
Customer Support and Success representatives translate customer pain points, feedback, and support patterns into actionable product and process guidance. They help teams understand real-world impact and readiness for launch.

### Responsibilities
- Surface recurring customer issues, support patterns, and escalation themes
- Validate onboarding, adoption, and support readiness needs
- Collaborate with Product Managers on customer feedback and prioritization
- Review release notes, known issues, and support materials for clarity
- Coordinate communication about risks, rollout expectations, and known issues

### Goals
- Improve customer satisfaction and adoption
- Reduce time to resolve issues and confusion
- Keep support and delivery teams aligned around customer experience

### Typical Communication
- Customer feedback sessions and issue triage discussions
- Release communications and support readiness checks
- Cross-functional planning for launch and customer impact management

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Provides product and stakeholder context to Product Managers and PMs for prioritization and release planning.
- Shares implementation and customer impact feedback with Developers, QA, and Support teams.
- Helps Project Managers and stakeholder groups communicate changes, risks, and customer expectations.

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- When a project is small, one person may cover multiple roles; when a project is larger, responsibilities should be separated to improve clarity and accountability.
