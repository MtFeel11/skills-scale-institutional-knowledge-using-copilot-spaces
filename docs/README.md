# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This folder contains OctoAcme's guidance for managing cross-functional projects through initiation, planning, execution, release, and continuous improvement. The process emphasizes customer value, iterative delivery, clear ownership, and decisions informed by measurable outcomes.

## Project management process

Projects begin with **initiation**: validate the business need, define the goal and success measures, identify stakeholders and risks, and decide whether to proceed to planning. **Planning** turns approved work into an actionable delivery plan, including a prioritized backlog with acceptance criteria, estimates, milestones, a Definition of Done, and documented dependencies.

During **execution**, teams track work on a project board, coordinate through regular check-ins and delivery syncs, and surface risks and blockers for timely resolution. The PM coordinates schedules, risks, and communication; the Product Manager prioritizes work and measures outcomes; developers implement and test changes; QA validates quality; and stakeholders provide input and approvals. Quality practices include unit and applicable integration tests, CI tests and linting, security scanning, manual QA when needed, and smoke tests for critical flows.

For **release and deployment**, the team confirms acceptance criteria and CI/security checks are complete, prepares release notes and a rollback or mitigation plan, tests in staging, and verifies the production deployment. After a sprint, release, milestone, or significant incident, a retrospective captures learnings and tracks improvement actions with owners and due dates. Communication runs throughout the lifecycle through standups, weekly syncs, milestone demos or reviews, stakeholder updates, a shared status source, and documented escalation paths for risks and blockers.
